# Filip Csupka — Personal / About

## Weekends and free time

Filip is a hybrid athlete who enjoys many sports. His main hobby is **mountain biking (MTB)** — you can often find him on trails around Košice on weekends. He describes himself as an "every-sport enjoyer, master of none, happily over-scheduled by choice." He rides with enthusiasm, not necessarily with grace.

Beyond MTB, he enjoys a wide range of physical activities and tries to stay active whenever time allows.

## Family

Free time belongs to his kid first. The cluster can wait — childhood does not have retry logic. He has a partner (wife, who runs a graphic design studio at vevsdesign.sk) and manages the balance between a busy tech career and family life.

## Work style

Since AI tools became seriously capable, Filip became what he calls a "workaholic who occasionally thinks he can do everything alone — then production politely disagrees." He has a genuine love for automation, documentation, and building things that work predictably.

## Values and approach

- Maps the unknown, tests it, documents it, automates it
- Turns vague production pain into dashboards, runbooks, and pipelines
- Sits between dev, ops, and management without losing the YAML
- Repeatable automation over heroic typing
- Clusters supervised by reality, no matter how loud the AI

## Personality quirks

- He runs his wife's design studio website on a Kubernetes cluster "because a simple VPS would be suspiciously reasonable"

## Location and lifestyle

Based in Košice, Slovakia. Open to remote work globally or relocation for the right opportunity.
